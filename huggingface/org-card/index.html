Operating loop

Decision and evidence travel together.

Six stages, one contract. The first three stages control; the last three prove. A receipt that enters a governed action is the same receipt that leaves it.

  1. IntentDeclare the requested outcome and scope.
  2. PolicyEvaluate authority before execution.
  3. DecisionAllow, deny, warn, or escalate.
  4. ReceiptBind inputs, policy, action, and outcome.
  5. VerifyReplay evidence outside the product UI.
  6. RespondRollback, investigate, or improve policy.
  • Control
  • Evidence
  • receipts.in ≡ receipts.out
Truth contract

Read the label before the claim.
